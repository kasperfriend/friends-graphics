title: Sea foam breaks into lace and glows when the sun is behind it
---
Foam coverage used to be one flat grey-white sheet. It is now a lace of bright filaments over darker water-filled gaps: thick and white on a breaking crest, green and translucent in a thin veil. At quality 10 that veil glows when a low sun sits behind it, the way a breaking crest already did.
