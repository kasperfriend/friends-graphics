title: Beaches now show water running up the sand and draining back out
---
The shoreline is read as a slope rather than as a distance, so a beach behaves like a beach and a rock wall like a wall. On a beach, sheets of water run up the sand with each arriving swell and drain back out again, taking their foam with them, and the wet band moves with them.
