title: Open water no longer shows the empty sky through it
---
Where the sheet has no geometry behind it - the open sea past a zone's barrier, a bay mouth - the surface now covers fully instead of showing 15% of the empty sky. Water over a seabed keeps its WATER OPACITY setting; 100 hides that too. Underwater the rule is skipped, so looking up shows the world.
