title: The world under the surface is now seen through the water, not through a flat fog

Below the surface, terrain and wrecks are lit through the same water the surface is: red dies first, the seabed carries the net the waves focus onto it, light comes down in beams, and the frame wobbles. Looking up, the water stops at the surface. Air haze and clouds fade out as the water fades in.
