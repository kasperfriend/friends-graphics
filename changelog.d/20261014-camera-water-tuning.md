title: Clouds, fog and torch lights stay anchored as you turn, and F7 can reset a tab
---
Clouds keep moving when you look straight up, and moonlight no longer leaves a black disk; foreground geometry still blocks clouds. Torch lights and local fog stay world-anchored as you turn. Water avoids redundant driver queries without reducing quality. Each F7 tab can reset and save its defaults.
