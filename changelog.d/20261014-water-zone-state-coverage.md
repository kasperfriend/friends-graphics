title: Water sea states now cover every map-root zone
---
Every root zone in the verified Ascension patch-M area database now has an explicit water setting: enclosed water stays calm, exposed seas stay livelier, and dry or development maps stay neutral. This custom WotLK 3.3.5-based table includes Ascension and expansion-derived map IDs; it is not a stock-client zone catalog. Child areas continue to inherit map, zone and area overrides.
