title: Water clarity, colours and wind can be authored per area
---
ClarityPercent, WindStrengthPercent, SparklePercent, WaveRoughnessPercent, SheenPercent, ShoalFoamPercent, FoamBrightnessPercent, SunDiscPercent, WaterColour, DeepColour and AbsorptionColour take per-area overrides, so a swamp and a mountain lake finally look different.
