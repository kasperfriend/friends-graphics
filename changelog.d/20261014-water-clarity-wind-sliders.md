title: Three new F7 water sliders: WATER CLARITY, WIND and SUN SPARKLE
---
WATER CLARITY sets how far light reaches before water goes to its deep colour, WIND decides how much sea state a body of water may carry (zero keeps a river glass, with no foam line), SUN SPARKLE sets how tight and dense the glitter on the sun road is.
