title: Water carries mid-sized waves between the long swell and the ripples
---
Between the long swell and the fine ripples the sea had nothing, and smooth waves at one fixed scale read as rows. A new band of mid-sized waves fills that gap. At quality 8 and above the water surface is lifted by them as well as shaded, so a crest you can see coming is a crest you can see arrive.
