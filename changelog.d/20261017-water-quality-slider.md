title: Water quality can now be dialled from flat to full detail
---
A new F7 WATER QUALITY slider, 0 to 10, trades the whole cost of the sea at once instead of one feature at a time. 0 is a flat sheet for a machine that is struggling, 6 is the water that shipped before, 7 is the default, 10 is everything. No level changes what any other water setting means.
