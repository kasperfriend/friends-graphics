title: Sun sparkle returns to water, and open water stops washing out toward the sun
---
Two terms in the new water shading had the wrong scale. Light scattering inside the column peaked on faces turned
toward the sun, flattening open water to one pale wash; the sun glint lost energy as its own lobe narrowed, leaving no
sparkle road at all. Both are normalized now.
