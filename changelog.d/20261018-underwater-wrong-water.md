title: No more underwater view on dry land
---
Teleporting somewhere with a raised lake in view (Mulgore) could turn the underwater view on while you stood on land, and it never turned off. The water now only counts the body you are actually in, forgets a lake once you travel away from it, and engages in zones where it never did (Loch Modan).
