title: Sun and moon rays now stream across the frame, cut by trees and characters
---
Sun and moon light now shows as visible shafts that reach across the sky, not only a glow around the disc. Trees, characters and buildings cast shadows along the beams, and clouds break them into gaps. Moon rays are stronger by default. Ray Strength in F7 scales both sun and moon rays; the moon ratio is MoonShaftPercent in GraphicsEffects.ini.
