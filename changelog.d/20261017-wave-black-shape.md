title: Waves no longer show a black shape through the water at surface height
---
A wave face turning away from the sun could be shaded past black, and because water is see-through and draws no depth, that face showed through the nearer one as a dark shape inside the wave. Shading is now floored above black, and the floor rises when light comes through the sheet toward you.
