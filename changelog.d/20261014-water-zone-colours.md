title: Water takes its colour from the zone it is in
---
Booty Bay is azure, Duskwood and the Plaguelands run dark, Winterspring and Icecrown are icy steel-blue, Zangarmarsh is murk, the Netherstorm violet — 45 places in all. Colours ease in at borders instead of snapping, and places without their own colours keep the global settings.
