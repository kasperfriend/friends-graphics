title: Volumetric clouds now drift across the sky
---
The sky now has a real lit cloud layer instead of a flat skybox. Clouds drift with the wind, are shaded by the sun and
moon, darken the light below them and thicken when it rains or snows. Coverage, height, thickness and quality are on
the F7 menu, and the whole layer can be switched off.
