title: Volumetric clouds now drift across the sky
---
The sky has a real lit cloud layer instead of a flat skybox. Clouds group and clear across the sky, shred at the
edges, haze out with distance and darken the ground under them. Coverage runs from a few puffs to a solid overcast
lid, and you can fly up through the deck and look down on it.
