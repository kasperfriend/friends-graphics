title: Waves hold their place in the world, travel at a natural pace, and stand up in 3D
---
The sea used to slide when the camera turned and boil along at any speed. It is nailed to the terrain now, crests cross at the pace their size says, and each wave lifts and leans as a body of water. Active water tears white foam on the crests that are breaking, and calm water stays glass.
