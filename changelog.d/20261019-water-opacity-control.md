title: Water has a real opacity setting
---
New WATER OPACITY slider (F7, Water tab, default 85) and [Water] OpacityPercent, per zone like the rest of the water. It is not clarity: clarity is how far light reaches through the body, opacity is how much of the scene the surface may leave showing. 0 restores the old see-through look.
