title: Plants lean plant by plant, and a mount no longer comes apart in the wind
---
Every bone of a plant now turns about its own position instead of the model's, so a patch of grass bends tuft by tuft and a tree's branches follow its trunk. A model that travels - a mount, a character, a creature - is refused by four separate witnesses (its placement point, its pose, its vertices, and whether any of them repeats) and stays refused while it moves, so saddles, armour and held objects no longer stretch with the wind while you ride. The debug rows count the plants a merged draw carries and name the first draw that leaned.
