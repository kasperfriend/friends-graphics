title: Only plants lean: gear, characters and mounts are never bent by the wind
---
Grass, bushes and trees bend bone by bone, so a merged patch of grass moves tuft by tuft. Shoulder armour, weapons, capes, saddles, characters, creatures and mounts never move: their bones are read for each draw and put back exactly as the client left them.
